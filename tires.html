Understanding Safety Ratings

Wet Grip (A–G)

Measures braking distance on wet roads. A is the best — means shorter stopping distances in rain.

A B C D E

Fuel Efficiency (A–G)

Rolling resistance rating. A-rated tires save fuel — up to 0.5L/100km difference between grades.

A B C D E

Noise (dB)

Measured in decibels. Lower dB = quieter ride. 1 sound wave (black bar) = very quiet. 3 bars = louder.
