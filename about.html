Our Story

Born from a Flat Tire at Midnight

Our founder, stuck on a highway with a blown tire at midnight, waited 3 hours for help. That night, TireXpress was born — a service built to eliminate that wait forever.

We assembled a team of ASE-certified technicians, equipped them with state-of-the-art mobile rigs, and built technology to connect them with drivers in need in under 60 minutes. Today, we serve thousands of customers across 25+ cities.
